Fit & positioning
Who do you work with?
Simono works with cybersecurity, cloud, data and AI companies, as well as specialist technology consultancies selling high-value B2B services or solutions. The best fit is a company with a strong offer that wants a more predictable way to reach senior decision-makers.
What makes Simono different from a traditional lead generation agency?
Simono does not simply deliver lists or run isolated campaigns. We build the complete outbound growth system: positioning, ideal customer profile, account selection, infrastructure, messaging, campaign execution, qualification, reporting and continuous optimisation. The objective is a repeatable commercial engine that your business can understand and scale.
Is outbound still effective?
Yes, but generic high-volume outreach is not. Effective outbound depends on relevant targeting, credible positioning, strong deliverability, useful messaging and disciplined follow-up. When those elements work together, outbound can create a consistent source of qualified conversations.
Why do you specialise in Cybersecurity, Cloud and Data?
These markets combine technical offers, complex buying groups and longer sales cycles. Simono's background in IT consulting and business development helps translate technical capabilities into clear commercial value for CTOs, CIOs, CISOs, Heads of Cloud, Heads of Data and other senior buyers.
Engagement & execution
How long does implementation take?
A new outbound foundation is normally designed and prepared for launch within two to four weeks. The exact timeline depends on your existing infrastructure, the complexity of your offer, the number of target segments and the channels selected.
Do you guarantee meetings?
We do not promise arbitrary meeting volumes before validating the offer, market and target audience. Simono focuses on qualified opportunities and transparent performance data. Where a meeting target is included in a specific engagement, the qualification criteria and conditions are agreed before launch.
We already have an SDR team. Can you still help?
Yes. Simono can strengthen an existing sales development function by improving targeting, deliverability, messaging, workflows, reporting and campaign strategy. We can build the operating system around your team rather than replacing it.
Which outbound channels do you use?
Depending on the market and engagement, the system can combine email, LinkedIn and telephone outreach. The channel mix is selected around the buyer, sales cycle and offer rather than forcing every company into the same sequence.
Markets & first month
Can you work internationally?
Yes. Simono supports specialist technology companies across Australia, Europe and North America. Targeting, messaging and campaign timing are adapted to each market and its buying culture.
What happens during the first month?
The first month focuses on strategy and foundation: clarifying the offer, defining the ICP, selecting target accounts, preparing data, configuring infrastructure, developing messaging and launching the first controlled campaigns. Initial signals are then used to refine positioning and execution.
